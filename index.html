FAQ

Good to know.

Is a laptop required for the AI Workshop?

No. A laptop is optional.

What is included with the AI Workshop?

Certificate, workshop kit, fun activity and refreshments. Lunch is not provided.

How many people can be in a Zero To One team?

Teams can have 1 to 5 members. The fee is ₹179 per person.

Is food provided during Zero To One?

No. Food is not provided.

How are judges allocated?

The planned allocation is 1 judge for every 10 teams. At 50 teams, that is 5 judges.

Where will registration happen?

The website will open the relevant Google Form. The final form links will be added later.
