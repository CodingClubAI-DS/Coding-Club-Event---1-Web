FAQ

Good to know.

Is a laptop required for the AI Workshop?

No. A laptop is optional. A laptop is recommended for hands-on activities during the workshop.

What is included with the AI Workshop?

Certificate, workshop kit, fun activity and refreshments. Lunch is not provided.

How many people can be in an AGENTICTHON team?

Teams can have 1 to 5 members. The fee is ₹200 per person.

Is food provided during AGENTICTHON?

No. Food is not provided.

How are judges allocated?

The planned allocation is 1 judge for every 10 teams. At 50 teams, that is 5 judges.
